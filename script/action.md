# intro call questions

quick presentation/ document that includes all these questions

## general

- what is the state of ai in your organization?
- what are you aiming to achieve with the hackathon and how can we help with this effort?

## location & lunch

- do they have a location?
- does this location offer enough space for all people?
- does this location have a beamer, digital whiteboard?
- can we connect via thunderbolt/ hdmi to the beamer/ whiteboard?
- can everyone see the beamer/ whiteboard?
- do you want pizza & cookies or something else?
- when do you want to lunch?
- if it starts at 9 we would enter the building at 8?
- is the room prepared or should we take care of that?
    - in which case we would like to be there a day before already
- can you send us a quick picture of the room just to get an impression of the space

## tools & challenge design

- do you already have agentic coding tools or should take care of that?
- do the people generally know how to use agentic coding tools?
- how technical are the people roughly?
- do the people have admin rights on their laptops or can they access virtual machines where they do?
- do you want the hackathon challenges to go in a specific direction or should we just build interesting tracks?

# specifics

## lunch (12 am or 1 pm)

- find a restaurant nearby that delivers to the location
- use codex/ google maps to get the restaurant phone number
- order lunch at least 2 days before the hackathon
- pizza order (~ 15 € per pizza, 1/3 magherita, 1/3 salami, 1/3 vegetarian)
    - 15 people -> 10 pizzas -> ~ 150 € -> 4 magherita, 3 salami, 3 vegetarian
    - 30 people -> 18 pizzas -> ~ 270 € -> 6 magherita, 6 salami, 6 vegetarian
    - 50 people -> 25 pizzas -> ~ 375 € -> 9 magherita, 9 salami, 7 vegetarian
- bowls order (~ 15 € per bowl)
    - 15 people -> 15 bowls -> ~ 225 €
    - 30 people -> 30 bowls -> ~ 450 €
    - 50 people -> 50 bowls -> ~ 750 €
- find 3 nearest rewe, check stock online and get beste wahl cookies for ~ 2 € ( 1/3 x[triple chocolate soft baked](https://www.rewe.de/produkte/rewe-beste-wahl-soft-cookies-triple-chocolate-210g/2916073/), 1/3 x [white choc lemon soft baked](https://www.rewe.de/produkte/rewe-beste-wahl-soft-cookies-white-choc-lemon-210g/8191124), 1/3 x [chocolate cream cookies](https://www.rewe.de/produkte/rewe-beste-wahl-chocolate-cream-cookies-210g/7679946))
    - 15 people -> 5 + 5 + 5 -> 30 €
    - 30 people -> 10 + 10 + 10 -> 60 €
    - 50 people -> 17 + 17 + 16 -> 100 €
- put the cookies into a reusable ikea bowl [GLADELIG, blau, ~ 6 €](https://www.ikea.com/de/de/p/gladelig-schuessel-blau-50586490/)
    - 15 people -> 5 bowls -> 30 €
    - 30 people -> 10 bowls -> 60 €
    - 50 people -> 15 bowls -> 90 €

we don't show that the cookies are from rewe or the bowls is from ikea!

thesis: most will just go with pizza & cookies
