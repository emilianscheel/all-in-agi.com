# Where to hand out the flyers

Start at Moosdorfstraße 10. Hand one flyer to a person at a company or a team office. Not to households, not to cafés, and not as a stack left on a counter.

This is a reading of public rules (Service Berlin, Verbraucherzentrale, BGH), not legal advice.

## Rules

- Sidewalk handouts on public street land need an Ordnungsamt permit, applied for 14 days ahead: [Werbematerial verteilen](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/326473/) (StrReinG § 8 Abs. 2). The fee is €34 plus €3 per street or Bezirk per day, or plus €5 for the whole city per day. The first walk is Treptow-Köpenick (`ordnungsamt@ba-tk.berlin.de`). Crossing into Friedrichshain-Kreuzberg or Neukölln means that Bezirk’s Ordnungsamt, or the citywide add-on. Stickers on cars are not allowed. Pick up litter the same day.
- Until that permit is in hand, walk into a business and hand one flyer to a person who takes it.
- A mailbox with no refusal sticker may legally receive an unaddressed flyer, household or business (§ 7 UWG; BGH 20.12.1988, VI ZR 182/88). Any visible refusal counts: “Keine Werbung”, “Bitte keine Werbung”, “Keine Reklame”, including a sticker on the building door. One ignore can support an injunction. Skip anything unclear.
- Still do not use mailboxes. Private households are not the buyer, and drops or unattended stacks are already out. At a business door, hand the flyer over only when someone accepts it.
- Lobbies, courtyards, coworking spaces, and the park are private or managed. Ask. If they say no, leave.

## Where to start

The buyer is a team, so rank streets by offices, not by how lively they are.

- **First hour, Alt-Treptow’s office edge, not the Kiez.** Elsenstraße 87–96 (multi-tenant offices in the old piano factory) and the commercial floors at An den Treptowers 1. An den Treptowers 3 is the Deutsche Rentenversicherung; do not work that security line. Moosdorfstraße 7–9 (Kreatur / MOOS) is the only stop on the home block: ask the host. Do not sweep the cafés on Karl-Kunger-Straße.
- **Same area, next.** Over the Elsenbrücke into the office buildings on Alt-Stralau, then the yards on Markgrafendamm toward Ostkreuz. Markgrafendamm 24 only through the coworking host. This is the useful part of Friedrichshain.
- **A separate trip.** Schlesische Straße, Falckensteinstraße, and Cuvrystraße (small agencies; St. Oberholz on Schlesische Straße). Not the shops on Boxhagener Platz, and not apartments in the Wrangelkiez.
- **Do not start in Neukölln.** Schillerkiez, Reuterkiez, Weserstraße, and Sonnenallee are homes, bars, and retail. A later transit stop such as KALLE at Karl-Marx-Straße 101 is optional only after the office walks above.

## How to walk

- Weekday late morning, and again just before offices close.
- One flyer per company. One variant per route, so `utm_content` stays comparable.
- Note the street and the count.
- Do not go back the same week.
